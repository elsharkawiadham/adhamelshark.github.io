Limits

These historical repository records do not show that every workflow was rerun for this portfolio. The checks do not verify unrelated GAIN Brain or EMO branches, future personal-data integrations, or recommendations. PDF search is lexical and limited to extractable text. Image-only scans require OCR, and a no-match result does not prove a concept is absent.
